# Narrative & Visual Design: "Morning Barakah & The Proverb"

## 1. Creative Vision & Concept

"Morning Barakah & The Proverb" (የጧት በረከት እና ምሳሌው) is an immersive, 72-second cinematic short film dedicated to the contemplative beauty of Ethiopian morning rituals. The core intention is to evoke tranquility, hospitality (*marhaban* / *astengido*), and the timeless transmission of wisdom through an intimate coffee ceremony (*jebena buna*).

---

## 2. Character Specification: Hajja Mariam (ሐጃ ማርያም)

- **Identity**: Fictional matriarch and cultural elder (~68 years old).
- **Physical Characteristics**:
  - Warm, deeply creased face radiating gentle grace and lived experience.
  - Expressive dark brown eyes reflecting firelight and morning dawn.
  - Hands displaying artisanal dexterity while tending coals and clay vessels.
- **Attire & Styling**:
  - Traditional hand-spun Ethiopian cotton gown (*habesha kemis*) with subtle embroidered trim (*tibeb*).
  - Fine white woven veil (*netela*) draped elegantly over shoulders and hair.
  - Modest silver filigree ear ornaments reflecting morning sunrays.
- **Demeanor**: Unrushed, composed, motherly, speaking in measured and affectionate tones (*lijey* / "my child").

---

## 3. Worldbuilding & Set Design

- **Location**: Historic stone courtyard with whitewashed mud plaster walls, merging elements of northern highland architecture with eastern walled city (*Harar Jugol*) textures.
- **Ceremonial Elements**:
  - **Clay Jebena**: Handcrafted black spherical clay pot with slender neck and delicate spout.
  - **Embers & Roaster (*Menkeshkesha*)**: Iron pan over charcoal stove (*madad*) with crackling green coffee beans turning glossy ebony.
  - **Aromatics**: Frankincense resin (*etan*) smoking atop hot charcoal inside a small clay censer (*girgira*).
  - **Flora & Herbs**: Scattered wild grasses (*ketema*) over woven straw mats; sprigs of fresh *tena'adam* (rue herb).
- **Lighting Palette**:
  - Soft golden hour dawn filtering through wooden shutters and courtyard trees.
  - Warm ember glow illuminating hands and steam.
  - Volumetric light rays cutting through fragrant incense and coffee vapor.

---

## 4. Linguistic & Narrative Arc

### The Amharic Proverb
> **"ቀስ በቀስ፣ እንቁላል በእግሩ ይሄዳል"**  
> *(Qas ba-qas, enqulal ba-egru yihedal)*  
> Literal: *"Slowly by slowly, even an egg walks on its own feet."*  
> Meaning: Through patience, diligence, and divine timing, the impossible unfolds naturally.

### Narrative Flow (8 Shots, ~72 Seconds)
1. **Shot 1 (0:00 - 0:09 | 9s)**: Establishing wide of the mist-shrouded highland sunrise and courtyard.
2. **Shot 2 (0:09 - 0:18 | 9s)**: Medium shot of Hajja Mariam lighting the charcoal stove (*madad*) with a soft smile.
3. **Shot 3 (0:18 - 0:27 | 9s)**: Close-up macro of roasting coffee beans on the *menkeshkesha*, fragrant smoke swirling.
4. **Shot 4 (0:27 - 0:36 | 9s)**: Hajja Mariam bringing the pan to the viewer/guest, waving the sweet aroma (*wafting the smoke*).
5. **Shot 5 (0:36 - 0:45 | 9s)**: High-angle medium of boiling water and grounded beans infusing inside the black *jebena*.
6. **Shot 6 (0:45 - 0:54 | 9s)**: Extreme close-up of the long, steady pour from high above into small handleless ceramic cups (*sini*).
7. **Shot 7 (0:54 - 1:04 | 10s)**: Hajja Mariam looking up warmly into camera, delivering the proverb and blessing.
8. **Shot 8 (1:04 - 1:12 | 8s)**: Elegant title card & AI disclosure card over gently fading embers and steam.

## 5. Audio Architecture & Sonic Palette

- **Voice Performance**: Warm, resonant, authentic elder Amharic accent; pacing at ~1.8 syllables per second to allow natural breathing space.
- **Diegetic Foley**:
  - Crisp snapping of charcoal embers.
  - Gentle rattling of green beans sliding on cast iron.
  - Rhythmic hiss of aromatic frankincense smoke.
  - Liquid stream splashing cleanly into delicate porcelain cups.
- **Musical Score**: Minimalist meditative traditional strings (single acoustic *krar* or bowing *masenqo*) blended with ambient atmospheric pads.
