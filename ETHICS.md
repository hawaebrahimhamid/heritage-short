# Ethical Guidelines & Cultural Stewardship: "Morning Barakah"

## 1. Principles of Cultural Integrity & Heritage Respect

The Ethiopian coffee ceremony (*buna*) and traditional oral wisdom (*tret ena messale*) are communal, living cultural heritage traditions recognized globally for their profound spiritual, social, and familial significance. This project applies generative AI not to replace human culture, but to celebrate and preserve oral heritage through creative homage.

### Core Ethical Commitments:
1. **Accurate Representation**: Ensure ceremonial instruments (the spherical *jebena*, *menkeshkesha*, *sini*, *madad*, and *itan*) are represented in historically accurate forms rather than generic exoticized facsimiles.
2. **Linguistic Dignity**: Protect the integrity of the Amharic language and Ge'ez script. Refuse garbled or hallucinatory text generation. All spoken proverbs must retain correct grammar, rhythm, and philosophical nuance.
3. **Respect for Elders**: Traditional matriarchs are custodians of wisdom. The character of Hajja Mariam must be depicted with dignity, warmth, and grace, avoiding caricature or sensationalism.

---

## 2. Character Ethics: Hajja Mariam (ሐጃ ማርያም)

- **Fictional Status**: Hajja Mariam is an entirely fictional composite character created solely for this artistic demonstration.
- **Non-Impersonation Guarantee**:
  - The visual generation seeds and voice synthesis models do **NOT** utilize or mimic the likeness, private image, or voice recording of any real living or deceased individual.
  - No private biometric data or uncredited voice actors were ingested without express consent.

## 3. Mandatory AI Disclosure & Provenance Standards

To combat synthetic disinformation and maintain transparent artistic boundaries, this project adheres to strict disclosure standards:

### 3.1 Shot 8: Dedicated Disclosure End Card
The final 8 seconds of the film are dedicated to a full-screen, unskippable disclosure card containing:
```text
Title: Morning Barakah & The Proverb (የጧት በረከት እና ምሳሌው)
Notice: This heritage vignette was produced using synthetic media and generative AI tools.
Character: "Hajja Mariam" is a fictional character created in tribute to Ethiopian oral storytelling traditions.
Language: Amharic narration with English subtitles.
Provenance & Audit Hash: Refer to runs.jsonl for complete generation metadata.
```

### 3.2 Content Credentials & Metadata
- Rendered MP4 files embed C2PA-compliant or standard XMP provenance metadata tags identifying the media as synthetically generated (`is_synthetic: true`).
- Distribution channels must include `#MadeWithAI` and explicit credit notes detailing the assistive role of machine learning tools.

---

## 4. Human-in-the-Loop Cultural Review Checklist

Before any render from `outputs/` is approved for public demonstration, it must pass the following four-point review:
- [ ] **Ceremony Protocol**: Is the sequence of roasting, wafting, boiling, and pouring culturally faithful?
- [ ] **Linguistic Review**: Does a fluent Amharic speaker confirm the proverb intonation and grammar?
- [ ] **Visual Sanity**: Are there zero mutated digits, distorted textiles, or anachronistic props?
- [ ] **End Card Presence**: Is the ethical AI disclosure card visible for at least 6 consecutive seconds?
